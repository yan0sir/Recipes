  - items:
      - quantity: "1"

        name: short-grain rice
        note: rinsed
      - quantity: "1"
        name: ripe tomato
        note: whole, core cut out
      - quantity: "2"
        unit: tbsp
        name: butter
      - quantity: "1 1/4"
        unit: cup
        name: water
      - quantity: "1/2"
        unit: tsp
        name: salt
      - name: black pepper
        note: to taste
steps:
  - Put the rice, water and salt in a small pot. Sit the tomato in the middle, cut side up.
  - Bring to a boil, cover, and cook on the lowest heat for 18 minutes.
  - Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
  - Add the butter, then crush the tomato into the rice with a spoon and stir. Finish with pepper.
notes: A fried egg on top makes it dinner.
