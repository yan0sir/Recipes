steps:
  - Brown the butter in a pan until it smells nutty and the bits turn golden. Pour into a bowl and cool 10 minutes.
  - Whisk in both sugars, then the egg and vanilla, until glossy.
  - Fold in the flour, baking soda and salt, then the chocolate.
  - Chill the dough 30 minutes. Heat the oven to 180°C.
  - Scoop 12 balls onto a lined tray and bake 10–12 minutes, until the edges are set. Top with flaky salt.
